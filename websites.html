Common questions.

What kind of businesses do you build websites for?

Small and local businesses, from trades and hospitality to retail and services, that need a site that works well on a phone and brings in enquiries.

Do you write the words?

Yes. We write the page copy with you, or polish what you already have.

Will it show up on Google?

We set up search optimisation, Google Search Console and your Google Business Profile so Google can find and list your site. Rankings depend on your area and your competition, so we don't promise positions.

Can you set up the domain and hosting?

Yes. We register or connect your domain, set up hosting and add the records your email needs.

What happens after launch?

We keep your site updated with changes and new content, so it stays current.
