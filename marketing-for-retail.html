Recent retail work.

A promo for Party Warehouse's move to a new Christchurch store, inviting locals to the opening evening, and a workshop video for On Cue, a Christchurch pool, snooker and darts specialist.

Give them a reason to visit.

New stock, events and offers only work if people see them.

We film your store, products and team, and turn them into short videos for Instagram and Facebook.

What's included.

  • New stock and product highlights
  • Store openings, events and sales
  • Your team and your shop floor
  • Reels and posts for Instagram and Facebook
  • Local Meta ads aimed at people near your store

Common questions.

Who is this for?

Shops and retail stores, from independent boutiques to larger stores, that want more locals to see what is on their shelves.

Do you film in store?

Yes. We film in your store at a time that suits you, so it works around your customers.

What do you post, and where?

Short videos and posts of your products, new stock, events, offers and team, on Instagram and Facebook. Paid campaigns are set up in Meta Ads Manager and aimed at people near your store.

Where do you work?

We are based in Christchurch and work across the city and the wider Canterbury region. We also travel across New Zealand.

How do we get started?

Send an enquiry through the contact form with a little about your business and what you would like help with. We reply within two working days.
