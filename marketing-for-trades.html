Made for the trades.

Short-form video, social media and local Meta ads for builders, sparkies, plumbers, landscapers and other trade businesses in Christchurch and Canterbury.

Let's talk about your marketing Christchurch, Canterbury and across New Zealand

Recent work for other local businesses.

Your work is the proof.

The best ad for a trade business is a finished job.

We come to site, film jobs as they happen and turn them into short videos and posts that show locals the standard of your work. You stay on the tools while we handle the filming, editing and posting.

What's included.

  • Jobs in progress and finished work
  • Before and after
  • Your team on site
  • Reels and posts for Instagram and Facebook
  • Local Meta ads for the areas you work in

Common questions.

Who is this for?

Trade businesses that want more local customers to see their work: builders, electricians, plumbers, landscapers, painters, roofers and similar trades.

Do you come to site?

Yes. We film on location, on site or at your workshop, on a normal working day.

What do you post, and where?

Short videos and posts of your jobs, your team and your finished work, on Instagram and Facebook. Paid campaigns are set up in Meta Ads Manager and aimed at the areas you work in.

Where do you work?

We are based in Christchurch and work across the city and the wider Canterbury region. We also travel across New Zealand.

How do we get started?

Send an enquiry through the contact form with a little about your business and what you would like help with. We reply within two working days.
