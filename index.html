Common questions.

What does Melu Media do?

Melu Media is a marketing company in Ōtautahi Christchurch. We film short-form video, manage Instagram and Facebook for businesses, run Meta ad campaigns and help with marketing strategy.

Where are you based, and where do you work?

We are based in Christchurch and work with businesses across the city and the wider Canterbury region. We also travel across New Zealand.

Do you film at our business?

Yes. We film on location at your premises, around your people, products and services, rather than in a studio.

Which platforms do you work with?

Instagram and Facebook. Paid campaigns are set up and managed in Meta Ads Manager, which covers both.

What kinds of businesses have you worked with?

Recent work includes hospitality, automotive and retail businesses.
