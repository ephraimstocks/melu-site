Common questions.

Who is this for?

Restaurants, cafés, bars, takeaways, bakeries and other hospitality businesses that want more locals to see what they serve.

Do you film at our venue?

Yes. We film at your venue at a time that suits you, so it works around service.

What do you post, and where?

Short videos and posts of your food, drinks, team and venue, on Instagram and Facebook. Paid campaigns are set up in Meta Ads Manager and aimed at people near you.

Where do you work?

We are based in Christchurch and work across the city and the wider Canterbury region. We also travel across New Zealand.
